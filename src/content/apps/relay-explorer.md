---
title: Relay explorer
description: Browse the events living on your relays — a read-only window into everything your strfry, nostr-rs-relay and chapar deployments are holding.
order: 1
---

The relay explorer connects to any relay you run (or any public one) and lets you browse what's actually stored there — events by kind, author, and recency.

Perfect for verifying a fresh relay is accepting events, or digging into what your whitelists and kind filters are letting through.
