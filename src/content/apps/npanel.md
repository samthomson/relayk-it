title: nPanel
description: Host nsites as normal websites on your own domain — publish from anywhere, nPanel serves them and keeps them fresh.
order: 3
---

nPanel is the easy way to run **nsites as real websites**: link a domain you own, publish your site to Nostr from any client, and nPanel serves it to visitors like any normal site.

- **Websites on your own domains** — pair it with the [nPanel gateway service](/services/npanel) and your nsite answers on `yourdomain.com`
- **Publish from anywhere** — update the site from any client; changes are picked up automatically, or refresh on demand
- **Built-in inspection** — read the NIP-5A manifests, file lists and paths a gateway serves, for debugging when a publish misbehaves
