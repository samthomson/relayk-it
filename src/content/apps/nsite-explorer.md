---
title: nsite explorer
description: Inspect and debug nsite data — manifests, file lists and published paths for any site on your gateways.
order: 3
---

The debugger's window into [nsite](/features/domains) publishing: read the **NIP-5A manifests** (kinds 15128/35128) behind any site, list the files they map to by path and hash, and see exactly what a gateway will serve.

Ideal for confirming a republish landed — compare the manifest's created-at and file hashes against what you pushed, without waiting on cache.
