Deploying is easy; knowing it *worked* is the hard part. RelayKit's insights check your running services and surface problems in the dashboard.

## What you get

- Per-service **status**, including degraded states (failed deploys no longer masquerade as "running")
- **Insights results** with severity — warn and critical findings are promoted to a top-level banner so you can't miss them ([0.3.0](/changelog))
- Quick access to **logs** for diagnosis
