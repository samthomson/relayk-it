---
title: Projects & environments
description: Organize services into projects and environments — group a relay, a Blossom and a gateway that belong together.
order: 5
---

Services don't live in one flat list. RelayKit mirrors Dokploy's model: **projects** contain **environments**, and each deployed service belongs to one.

Use it however suits you — a project per product (`my-relay-stack`), or per context (`production` / `staging` environments inside one project).
